Multilingual Emotion Detection | Python, PyTorch, Hugging Face Transformers, scikit-learn, LIME

Developed a multi-label emotion classification deep learning model for English, Hindi, and Marathi text using DistilBERT and mBERT architectures.  

Increased the English model's F1-macro score from a 0.372 baseline to 0.620, and achieved a 0.811 F1-macro score on the multilingual dataset.  

Handled dataset class imbalance by implementing custom loss functions, specifically Focal Loss and Weighted BCE, to improve detection accuracy across all emotion categories.  

Added interpretability features using LIME and attention heatmaps to visually highlight the specific words and tokens driving the model's predictions
